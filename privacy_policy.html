Privacy Policy for Cannon

Last Updated: October 17, 2026
Summary: Cannon is a private automated publishing workflow tool used by authorized administrators to schedule and publish video reels to owned Facebook Pages. We do not collect, harvest, track, sell, or share any personal user data.

1. Information We Collect

Cannon only interacts with data required to execute authorized social media publishing:

  • Page Access Tokens: Temporarily utilized to authenticate with Meta Graph API endpoints to publish video reels on behalf of connected Pages.
  • Media & Content: Video files, descriptions, closed captions (.srt), and high-CTR thumbnail images authored by page owners for publishing.
  • Usage Data: We do not collect personal browsing data, device tracking data, GPS locations, or personal identity information from Facebook users or followers.

2. How We Use Information

Information processed by Cannon is used strictly for the following purposes:

  • Executing automated and scheduled video uploads to authorized Facebook Pages via official Meta Graph API v21.0 protocols.
  • Attaching automated closed captions (.srt) and Meta AI-generated content disclosures to published video reels.
  • Logging local publishing history (timestamp, post ID, video ID) for anti-ban rate limiting and spam prevention.

3. Data Sharing & Third Parties

We do not sell, rent, trade, or distribute any user data or credentials to third parties or advertising networks. Content is transmitted exclusively to Meta’s verified endpoints (graph.facebook.com and rupload.facebook.com) under official developer protocols.

4. Data Storage & Security

All sensitive credentials and Page access tokens are stored securely in local encrypted configuration stores and are never transmitted to unauthorized external systems.

5. User Data Deletion Instructions

In accordance with Meta Platform Rules and GDPR/CCPA regulations, users and administrators have the right to request deletion of all associated data:

  • To revoke Cannon's access to your Facebook Page, navigate to your Facebook Profile > Settings & Privacy > Settings > Business Integrations, locate Cannon, and click Remove.
  • To request immediate deletion of any cached page identifiers or configuration logs, please email our Data Protection contact at zakysocial2026@hotmail.com with the subject line "Data Deletion Request". All associated local records will be permanently purged within 48 hours.

6. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any revisions will be reflected with an updated date at the top of this page.
