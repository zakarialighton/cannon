Cannon - User Data Deletion Instructions

Cannon is an automated publishing application for Facebook Pages. We do not store personal data from Facebook users. If you have interacted with or authorized Cannon on your Facebook account and wish to delete your data, please follow the steps below:

Method 1: Remove App Access via Facebook (Instant)
  1. Log in to your Facebook account.
  2. Go to Settings & Privacy > Settings.
  3. In the left sidebar, click Business Integrations (or Apps and Websites).
  4. Find Cannon in the list.
  5. Click Remove and confirm. Facebook will immediately revoke all access and cease sharing any information with Cannon.
Method 2: Email Data Purge Request

To request a manual purge of any page configurations, publishing history logs, or access tokens associated with your page, send an email to:

Email: zakysocial2026@hotmail.com
Subject: Cannon - Data Deletion Request

Include your Facebook Page ID or Name in the request. All records will be completely removed from our local storage within 48 hours, and a confirmation receipt will be emailed back to you.
